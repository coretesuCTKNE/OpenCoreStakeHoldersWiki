# PLU Salience

The PLU framework classifies stakeholders by three attributes:

- **Power** — Ability to influence firm outcomes
- **Legitimacy** — Right to demand or expect
- **Urgency** — Time-sensitivity of claims

Combinations yield 8 salience classes: Definitive, Dominant, Dangerous, Dependent, Dormant, Discretionary, Demanding, Latent.

*(Mitchell, Agle & Wood 1997)*
