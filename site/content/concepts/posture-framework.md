# Posture Framework

The Posture Framework describes the firm's strategic stance toward a stakeholder, derived from the assessment of cooperative and harmful potential. Posture is not fixed — it should be recalibrated at each Reconciliation pass.

## Dimensions

### Cooperative Potential (CP)

The degree to which the stakeholder can contribute to the firm's goals.

Scored 1–10:

- **1** — no cooperation possible
- **10** — fully aligned, maximum synergy

### Harmful Potential (HP)

The degree to which the stakeholder can threaten or harm the firm's goals.

Scored 1–10:

- **1** — no threat
- **10** — existential threat to firm survival

## Posture Types

| Posture       | CP  | HP  | Strategy                                          |
| ------------- | --- | --- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Offensive** | ≥6  | ≤4  | Proactive partnership; invest in the relationship |
| **Defensive** | ≤4  | ≥6  | Minimal engagement; monitor closely               |
| **Swing**     | ≥6  | ≥6  | Careful cultivation; contingency planning         |
| **Hold**      | ≤4  | ≤4  | Re-evaluate; insufficient data or low priority    |

### Posture Derivation

```
Offensive  → CP >= 6 AND HP <= 4
Defensive → CP <= 4 AND HP >= 6
Swing     → CP >= 6 AND HP >= 6
Hold      → CP <= 4 AND HP <= 4
```

> **Note:** A Normative engagement basis with Hold posture is a theoretical contradiction — flag for human review. Ethical obligation and strategic inactivity are incompatible.

## Behavior Notes

Describe specific cooperative or threatening behaviors observed from this stakeholder:

- What cooperative behaviors have they demonstrated?
- What threatening signals have they sent?
- How do these align with the CP/HP scores?

## Posture vs. Salience

Posture and salience are related but distinct:

- **Salience** — stakeholder's characteristics (what they have)
- **Posture** — firm's strategic stance (what we do)

A Definitive stakeholder (high salience) does not automatically receive an Offensive posture — the firm must still assess cooperative and harmful potential independently.
